How it works

Three steps, from one door to the other.

1

Tell us about the car

Both addresses, the day the car is ready, and the car: make and model, size, whether it drives, its value and one photo.

2

Pick one of three prices

Saver, Standard or Priority, each with its pickup window, a deliver-by date and one total. You pay, and Car2Door gives the job to a checked transport company on your route.

3

Picked up, checked, delivered

At pickup and delivery the driver photographs the car with you and you both sign. In between you follow your car on the map and chat with the driver in your language.

Questions

How is the price set?

From the distance, the car's size, whether it drives and how soon it has to be picked up. You see three totals before you pay, and the one you pick stays as it is, as long as the car is as you described it.

When do I see who carries my car?

As soon as a transport company accepts, the app shows the company, the driver and the vehicle, and you can chat or call them. On the day you follow your car on the map.

What if no transport company takes my car?

You get your money back in full, automatically.

Can I cancel?

Yes. How much you get back depends on how close to pickup you cancel. See the terms for clients.
