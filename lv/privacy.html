Privacy Policy

For the Car2Door and Car2Door Pro apps and car2door.eu, run by StepsNest B.V.
Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

StepsNest B.V. ("Car2Door", "we"), Weert, the Netherlands, KvK 42105882 (its registered office under Company details below), is the controller for the personal data in this policy. Questions or requests: support@car2door.eu. We have no data protection officer; the law doesn't require one for a business of our size and kind.

Two others decide about some of the same data themselves, as separate controllers:

  • The transporter that accepts a trip, for the job data it gets (section 6). Its own privacy notice applies to what it does with it.
  • Stripe, for its payment, fraud and identity checks (section 6).

2. Whose data, and where it comes from

  • Clients who book, from what they enter.
  • Contacts at pickup and delivery (for example the seller and the recipient): the client gives us their name and phone; they add their signature and a code at the handover.
  • Transporters and the person who signs up for them, from what they enter, the company registers, VIES and Stripe.
  • Drivers, from their transporter and from what they do in the app.
  • Visitors of car2door.eu, and people who send the Join Transporters form.

4. Photos, the registration paper, plates and VINs

  1. The registration paper: only the vehicle side (Part I), with the holder's name and address covered; never Part II. Only our staff see the photo; the transporter gets the VIN, make, model and weight read from it. The photo is deleted 30 days after delivery, or when the booking is cancelled.
  2. The four corner photos at pickup and delivery are taken with the app's camera, with time and place. The client, the transporter and their insurers see them. Faces and plates may show; we do no face recognition.
  3. VINs and plates are personal data while they can be linked to a person. We use them for the booking, the consignment note and checks against stolen cars and duplicate bookings.
  4. The car's papers read on the phone: when the app fills the booking from a photo of the registration paper, the reading happens on your phone; that photo doesn't leave it for the reading.

5. Location during a transport

  1. While the driver heads to the pickup, Car2Door Pro shares the driver's position with that client, so they see the driver coming, also when the phone is locked.
  2. From pickup to delivery it goes on, but the client, and the recipient on the delivery link page, see only the kilometres left, rounded to 50 km (to 10 km in the last 100 km), never a town, and the exact point only within the last 30 km before delivery. The client also sees the day the car is expected to arrive, worked out from the kilometres left: an estimate, not a promise; the deliver-by date stays the commitment. A van that stands still sends nothing: after 20 minutes without a new position they see only "On a break". Car2Door Pro tells the driver when the position is shared.
  3. We keep only the latest position, never a trail of where the car has been. It is deleted at delivery, when the trip is given back or cancelled, or 3 hours after the last one.
  4. The client's app asks for location only when you choose to use your position for an address.

6. Who we share it with

Separate controllers

  • The transporter that accepts a trip: before it accepts, the cities, the dates, the car's make, model and value band, and a small photo of the car; after, the addresses, the contacts' names and phones, the VIN and the car's data, the photos and the chat. It uses them for the job and its own legal duties (such as its tax records and the consignment note).
  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe Ltd., Ireland, and Stripe, Inc., USA): payments, refunds and payouts, and the identity check of the person who signs up a transporter. Stripe uses the data also for its own fraud and legal checks, under its own privacy policy.
  • Apple and Google, when you sign in with them.
  • Tax offices and authorities, when the law requires: the Dutch tax office (DAC7, invoices), and others on a lawful request.

Processors, who work for us under a data processing agreement

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.): our database, sign-in, file storage and server functions, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), EU region: error reports, sent without names, emails or ids.
  • Lettermint (Lettermint B.V., the Netherlands): the emails the apps send.
  • GatewayAPI (Denmark), once the phone code is on: the text messages with your phone code.
  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC): our mailbox support@car2door.eu (Google Workspace); the translation of chat messages when it is on (Google Cloud Translation, EU endpoint); the encrypted copy of our backups (Google Drive).
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA): app updates, and push notifications together with Apple (iPhone) and Google (Android).
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA): hosts car2door.eu and keeps visitors' IP addresses in its logs for security.

Services your phone or browser calls directly

  • Address search: Photon (Komoot GmbH, Germany) and, as a fallback, Nominatim (the OpenStreetMap Foundation, United Kingdom) get the address you type, or your position when you ask for it.
  • Maps: in the apps, Apple Maps on iPhone and Google Maps on Android load the map around the points shown. On car2door.eu the map is an OpenStreetMap frame (the OpenStreetMap Foundation), which gets the map area shown, including the driver's position when you follow a trip.
  • Stripe.js: Stripe's card form, loaded only on the card step; Stripe reads signals from your browser or phone against fraud.
  • Navigation: a driver who taps Navigate leaves the app for Google Maps, Apple Maps or Waze.

Registers we look things up in: the EU's VIES (VAT numbers); Romania's ANAF; Poland's KRS and its VAT white list; Lithuania's and Latvia's open company data; the EU and Dutch sanctions lists, downloaded so no name is sent out.

We never sell personal data and we show no ads.

7. Transfers outside the EU

Supabase, Stripe, Sentry, Expo, Apple, Google and GitHub are US companies or use US services. Each transfer rests on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the company is certified, or on the EU's standard contractual clauses. The OpenStreetMap Foundation is in the United Kingdom, which the EU recognises as protecting data adequately. Ask us for a copy of the safeguards.

9. Automated decisions

Our fraud signals, the register checks and the counters of a transporter's late give-backs only flag: a person on our team decides whether any hold, pause or refusal goes on, and you can ask for the reasons and for a review.

A few steps are automatic, because a law, a fact or your own step decides them, not our judgement:

  • a possible match on a sanctions list holds the booking, the transporter's jobs or the payout until a person on our team clears it: sanctions law forbids making money available to a listed person;
  • a transporter gets no new jobs while a paper has expired or is missing, while it has not accepted the current terms, or while its company is outside the EU, and a VAT number VIES no longer knows stops counting for its country: these are facts we must check, or the transporter's own step;
  • Stripe's own checks can stop a payment or a payout: that is Stripe's decision (section 6).

A person on our team looks at any of them when you ask, in the app's chat or at support@car2door.eu.

10. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and for a copy to take elsewhere: in the app's chat, or at support@car2door.eu. We answer within one month. You can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl), or to the authority where you live.

11. Deleting your account

  1. In the app: Profile › Delete account. If you can't sign in, email support@car2door.eu from your account's email address.
  2. Your account is frozen at once: you are signed out everywhere, your profile is hidden, and no new bookings, jobs or marketing reach you. It is deleted by itself 28 days later.
  3. We email you at once with that date, what we keep and why (point 5), and a link to undo it. Until that date you can undo it, or choose "Delete now" to skip the wait. When it is done, we email you again.
  4. If a trip of yours is under way, a payout, a refund or an invoice is still due, or a damage report or a payment dispute (chargeback) about one of your trips is open, nothing is deleted before the deletion day either: until then the account is only frozen, and "Keep my account" brings all of it back. On that day (day 28, or at once with "Delete now") we delete what can go, and what the trip, payout, refund, invoice, report or dispute still needs when it ends (for a report or a dispute, as the law allows for a legal claim). We tell you within one month what was done and what waits.
  5. What we keep after deletion, and why (section 8): invoices and their payment records (7 years, tax law); DAC7 details (7 years); a trip's evidence while a claim can still be made, and a paid trip's details with its invoices; reports about content and our decisions (1 year); a phone's id used with an invite code (1 year); the record that the account was deleted (1 year, so that a restored backup can't bring it back); our staff's decisions on a transporter's checks; and sanctions hits: a confirmed hit with the name, as proof of the screening sanctions law requires, any other without it.

12. Security

Your data is stored encrypted in Frankfurt. Only staff who need it see it, with two-step sign-in, and staff actions are logged. Card numbers stay with Stripe. If a breach puts you at risk, we tell you.

14. Drivers

If you drive for a transporter: your transporter gives us your name, licence class and expiry, and a photo of your licence; we check them before you get jobs. The client and their contacts see your name and the plates 24 hours before pickup. From the way to the pickup until delivery, the app shares your position with that client; between pickup and the last 30 km the client and the recipient see only the kilometres left, and nothing while the van stands still. Only the latest position is kept (section 5). Your signature and the photos you take go into the trip's record. Your rights are in section 10.
