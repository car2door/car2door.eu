Terms of Service for clients

For the Car2Door app and car2door.eu, run by StepsNest B.V.
Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Who we are

Car2Door is run by StepsNest B.V., Weert, the Netherlands, Chamber of Commerce (KvK) 42105882; its registered office and VAT number are under Company details below. Email: support@car2door.eu. In these terms "Car2Door", "we" and "us" mean StepsNest B.V.; "you" means the person or business that books.

2. What Car2Door is, and is not

  1. Car2Door is an online platform where independent transport companies ("transporters") take car transports that clients book.
  2. We act as the transporters' agent. We book your transport openly in the name of the transporter that accepts it, and we take your payment for it.
  3. We do not carry cars. We are not the carrier, not your forwarder and not an insurer.
  4. Car2Door never collects the price of the car itself.

3. Two contracts

  1. These terms, between you and Car2Door, for using the app and the website. They cost you nothing.
  2. The transport contract, between you and the transporter. It starts when a transporter accepts your booking. It follows the transport conditions (car2door.eu/transport-conditions) and the CMR Convention, the international law for road transport.

4. What Car2Door does for you

  1. We check every transporter before its first job, and again on a schedule:
    • the company in its country's company register, and that the person who signs up is a director there, matched with Stripe's identity check;
    • its VAT number in the EU's VIES check (or, for a small business, its national tax number);
    • its EU Community licence for road transport;
    • its CMR insurance: the certificate and the schedule, read against the company, the countries of your trip, the sum (at least €50,000 per event; €250,000 for a vehicle with more than 3 car places) and the dates;
    • for a car worth more than €15,000: cover for the car's full value;
    • the EU and Dutch sanctions lists, for the company, its account holder and its drivers.
  2. What a check means: these papers were valid on the day we checked them. It is not a promise about how the transporter or its drivers drive or work.
  3. We also: run the booking and its dates; offer your job to checked transporters; take, hold, pass on and refund your money as these terms say; pass messages and documents between you and the transporter; keep the trip's record (photos, signatures, the consignment note); help you with a claim (section 17); and protect your data (our Privacy Policy).

5. Your account

  1. You must be 18 or older, give true details and keep your sign-in safe. One account per person or business.
  2. When the app asks, you confirm your phone number with a code we text you.
  3. If you book for a business, give its VAT number (checked in VIES) and its name and address for the invoice.

6. Booking

  1. Routes: between countries in the EU, on the routes the app offers. Some routes have conditions the app shows before you book.
  2. What we carry: cars, SUVs and vans up to 3.5 tonnes, that drive or not. Not wrecks, damaged electric cars or motorcycles.

  3. Options and dates. Each option has a pickup window and a deliver-by date, shown before you book:

    • Saver: pickup within a 7-day window;
    • Standard: pickup within a 5-day window;
    • Priority: pickup on your chosen day or the next.

    The transporter picks the pickup day inside the window. Your car is delivered by the deliver-by date.

  4. Booking is a request. Your booking becomes a contract only when a transporter accepts it (section 10).

7. The car and what you tell us

  1. You give the car's make and model from the list, its VIN (chassis number), its value band (up to €15,000; €15,000 to €50,000; over €50,000), whether it drives or rolls, and one photo of the whole car.
  2. You upload a photo of the vehicle side of the registration paper (Part I), with your name and address covered, at the latest 72 hours before the pickup window. A Priority booking made later than that uploads it when booking. Without it, no job is offered and the booking is cancelled at no cost. Never send Part II (the ownership part); it does not travel in the car.
  3. With your booking you confirm: you own the car or may have it moved; it is not stolen; nothing personal is inside, only the car's own equipment.
  4. If the car is not as booked (another car, a VIN that doesn't match, a car that doesn't drive when you said it does), the driver may refuse to load it. That counts as a no-show (section 13).

8. Price

  1. Each option shows one total. For a private client it includes the VAT of the pickup country, which the transporter charges. A business client pays the net price; the VAT is reverse charged, unless you and the transporter are in the same country, which shows when it accepts.
  2. The price comes from Car2Door's tariff. As the transporters' agent we set the most a transporter may charge. A transporter may accept for less; you then pay the lower price.
  3. The price is fixed once you book. Nothing is added later, except as the transport conditions say. There is no fee for paying by card.

9. Payment and invoices

  1. You pay Car2Door, which collects the payment for the transporter. Paying Car2Door counts as paying the transporter. Stripe, our payment provider, handles the card; we never see your full card number.
  2. Your card is saved when you book and charged when a transporter accepts. If the charge fails or your bank asks you to confirm it, you have 12 hours to fix it in the app; otherwise the booking is cancelled at no cost.
  3. Your invoice is the transporter's. Car2Door issues it in the transporter's name and on its behalf, with the VAT the transporter charges, and emails it to you.
    • Where the law of the transporter's country needs the invoice to come from the transporter itself, through that country's e-invoice system (such as KSeF in Poland, e-Factura in Romania or Online Számla in Hungary), the transporter issues it there, and we send you a receipt for your payment.
  4. Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Stripe.
  5. Never pay a driver or a transporter anything outside the app. If anyone asks, tell us.

10. When a transporter accepts

  1. Your job is offered to checked transporters whose vehicles and papers fit your trip. Those with a planned run on your route see it first. The first to accept gets it.
  2. When one accepts, your transport contract with it starts, on the transport conditions. Your trip in the app then shows the transporter's name, VAT number, Community licence and CMR insurer.
  3. 24 hours before pickup you see the driver's name and the vehicle's plates.
  4. If a transporter accepts less than 48 hours before your pickup window starts, you can cancel free within 2 hours of being told who it is, until its driver sets off for the pickup (section 13).

11. If no transporter accepts

  1. After an hour without a taker the app tells you, and you can cancel free at any time until a transporter accepts.
  2. If no one accepts before the pickup window ends, the booking ends and nothing is charged.

12. Pickup, inspection and delivery

  1. Who hands over: you, or the contact you name (for example the seller). They get a 6-digit pickup code from us. They check the driver's face against an ID (looking only, no copy) and the plates against those in the app. If something doesn't match, they press "Don't hand over" and keep the car.
  2. Inspection: at pickup the driver takes four photos with the app's camera, one at each corner of the car, plus a close-up of each damage mark, and ticks that the VIN on the car is the one booked. The driver and whoever hands over both look and sign: "I saw these photos and the notes. Signing is not admitting fault." Old damage is written down in words. No match of the VIN, no loading.
  3. The consignment note: an electronic CMR note in the app, with you as sender and the transporter as carrier. For a pickup in Belgium it is printed on paper.
  4. Waiting: the driver waits at least 60 minutes at pickup and at delivery after calling and messaging your contact.
  5. Delivery: only to the recipient you named, against the delivery code they give the driver. The driver takes the same four photos; the recipient looks and signs, and writes any new damage on the delivery report. If nobody is there, the transporter keeps the car safe and a new delivery is agreed in the trip chat.
  6. Only you change the delivery address or the recipient, and only in the app.

13. If you cancel

Before a transporter accepts: free; nothing is charged.

After a transporter accepts, counted to the start of the pickup window:

  • 48 hours or more before: all back;
  • 12 to 48 hours before: 50% back;
  • under 12 hours before, or a no-show: nothing back.

If the transporter accepted less than 48 hours before the window starts, you can first cancel free within 2 hours of being told who it is, as long as its driver hasn't set off for the pickup; after those 2 hours, or once the driver has set off, the steps above apply.

The part kept goes to the transporter for the slot it kept for you.

14. If the transporter cancels, or we stop a booking

  1. If a transporter gives your job back, it goes to other checked transporters at the same price and dates. If none takes it in time, you choose new dates or a full refund.
  2. We may stop a booking for a sanctions hit, signs of fraud, a missing registration paper or a car we don't carry. You then get all your money back, unless you caused it with false details.

15. No 14-day right of withdrawal

Your transport has fixed dates, so the EU's 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply (Directive 2011/83/EU, Article 16(l)). You can cancel as section 13 says.

16. Damage, loss and delay: your claim is against the transporter

  1. The transporter answers for loss of or damage to your car from pickup to delivery, and for delay, under the CMR.
  2. The limit: under the CMR the transporter's liability is limited to 8.33 SDR (about €10) per kilogram of the car, unless the transporter acted wilfully. For a 1,500 kg car that is about €15,000. This is not insurance of the car's full value. For a car worth more than €15,000, the transporter answers up to the car's value, which is written on the consignment note.
  3. Deadlines (CMR Articles 30 and 32):
    • visible damage: write it on the delivery report at delivery;
    • damage you could not see then: tell the transporter in writing within 7 days of delivery, Sundays and public holidays not counted. A report in the app counts;
    • delay: in writing within 21 days of delivery, or no compensation for it;
    • a court claim: within one year (three years for wilful misconduct). A written claim pauses that year until the transporter rejects it in writing.

17. How Car2Door helps with a claim

  1. Report damage in the app. We send your report to the transporter the same day and answer you within 2 working days. A report in the app counts as your written notice to the transporter.
  2. We give you the transporter's CMR insurer and the trip's photos, signatures and consignment note.
  3. If you report before the transporter is paid for the trip, we hold its payment for up to 30 days. If the transporter or its insurer accepts your claim in that time, we pay you from it.
  4. We don't decide who is liable. The transporter and its insurer do, or a court.

18. Car2Door's own liability

  1. We answer for our own duties in sections 4 and 9: the checks as described, the booking, your money and the information we give.
  2. We do not answer for the carriage itself: that is the transporter's (section 16).
  3. For a private client our liability for our own mistakes is limited to the top of your car's value band: €15,000 for a car up to €15,000, and €50,000 for the other bands. For a business client it is limited to the price of the booking.
  4. These limits never apply to intent, gross negligence, death or injury, or where the law does not allow a limit.

19. Messages, reviews and reports

  1. After a transporter accepts, you and it can chat in the app. Be polite; no illegal content; no arranging payments or trips outside the app. When translation is on, messages are translated automatically.
  2. After a completed trip, the client who booked can review the driver and the company once, 1 to 5 stars with an optional comment. Reviews come only from completed trips in the app; nobody is paid for one. We don't remove a review for a low rating. We remove content that is illegal or breaks these terms, and tell its author why.
  3. Anyone can report a message, review or photo they think is illegal: press and hold it in the app, or write to support@car2door.eu. A person on our team looks at each report. Authorities can reach us at support@car2door.eu, in English or Dutch.

20. Questions and complaints

Write in the app's chat or to support@car2door.eu. We answer the chat Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 20:00 Central European Time, usually within 2 hours. If we can't solve a complaint, you can go to the court named in section 23.

21. Your data

How we use your data is in our Privacy Policy (car2door.eu/privacy). The transporter that accepts your trip gets what it needs for the job and uses it as its own controller.

22. Ending your account, and changes to these terms

  1. You can delete your account in the app at any time. It is frozen at once and deleted after 28 days, or at once if you choose "Delete now". What a trip under way, a refund or an invoice still due, or an open damage report or payment dispute needs is deleted when it ends, as the Privacy Policy says (section 11).
  2. We may suspend an account only for the reasons in these terms (false details, fraud, abuse of others, a sanctions hit), and we tell you why.
  3. We tell you about changes to these terms by email and in the app at least 15 days before they apply. New terms apply to bookings made after that day; a booking keeps the terms it was made under.

23. Law and courts

  1. These terms follow Dutch law. If you are a private client, you keep the protection of the law of the country where you live, and you can take us to court there; we can take you to court only there.
  2. Business clients: the court in Limburg (Rechtbank Limburg), the Netherlands.
  3. The transport contract follows the transport conditions: Dutch law, the CMR, and the courts the CMR allows.

24. Languages

These terms are in English. When they are published in another language, each language version is equally valid.
