Cookie Policy

For car2door.eu and the Car2Door apps, run by StepsNest B.V.
Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. What this covers

Cookies and similar storage: small items a website or an app keeps on your device. car2door.eu sets no cookies of its own. It keeps a few items in your browser's storage, only those needed for what you ask the site to do. We use no analytics, no advertising and no tracking across sites.

2. What car2door.eu keeps in your browser

Item What it does How long
car2door.language the language you chose until you clear it
car2door.language-asked that the language question was shown this visit
car2door-check tests that storage works, removed at once a moment
car2door-join-draft your Join Transporters answers until you send them until sent or cleared
sb-…-auth-token, car2door.profile, car2door-name, car2door.sign-in keep you signed in at /book and show your first name in the header until you sign out
car2door.oauth, car2door.frame-return, car2door.booking-link at /book: that a sign-in with Google or Apple is under way and the page to come back to, and the trip you started on the site's booking card this tab, until used
car2door.booking your booking answers, so you don't lose them if the page closes until booked or cleared
car2door-referral an invite code from a link (only when invites are offered; off today) this visit

3. Third parties the site loads

  • Stripe (stripe.com): its card form loads only on the card step of a booking. Stripe sets its own cookies there to prevent fraud; they are needed for the payment. Stripe's cookie policy: stripe.com/cookie-settings.
  • Apple and Google, if you choose to sign in with them: you go to their page, under their own cookie rules.
  • OpenStreetMap (openstreetmap.org): the map frame on a trip page, which loads map images.
  • Photon (photon.komoot.io) and Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org): the address search gets what you type.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site, logs IP addresses for security.

None of them gets a cookie from car2door.eu.

4. In the apps

The Car2Door and Car2Door Pro apps keep on your phone: your sign-in, your language, a booking you haven't finished, payment and pickup steps in progress, photos waiting for a signal, the push token, and, in Car2Door Pro during a trip, which trip shares your position. Sentry gets error reports without names. Nothing is used for ads or tracking.

5. Your choices

You can clear the site's storage in your browser's settings at any time; you then choose your language again and sign in again. Blocking Stripe's cookies stops card payments.

6. Changes

If we ever add something that needs your consent, we ask first, and this page says so.
