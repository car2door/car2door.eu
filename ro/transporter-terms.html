Terms of Service for transporters

For the Car2Door Pro app, run by StepsNest B.V.
Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Who we are, and our roles

  1. Car2Door Pro is run by StepsNest B.V., Weert, the Netherlands, KvK 42105882, support@car2door.eu ("Car2Door", "we"); its registered office and VAT number are under Company details below.
  2. "You" are the transport company that uses Car2Door Pro. You carry the cars. For every job you are the carrier under the CMR and the client's contracting party.
  3. We act as your agent: we book jobs in your name, collect the client's payment for you and do the other things in section 6. We are not the carrier, a forwarder or an employer, and we carry no liability for the transport.

2. Who may join

  1. A transport business established in the EU, registered in its country's company register (a one-person business such as a Romanian PFA or a Polish JDG too), with an EU Community licence for the international carriage of goods by road.
  2. The person who signs up must be a director or the owner in the register. Stripe checks that person's identity when you set up payouts; we match it with the register.
  3. You give an EU VAT number that shows in VIES. A small business without one may give its national tax number instead, and then gets jobs from business clients in its own country only, until its VAT number comes.

3. Your documents and our checks

  1. You upload, and keep valid: the Community licence and a certified copy for each vehicle; your CMR insurance (certificate and schedule); for cars over €15,000, your full-value cover; and the papers each vehicle and driver needs in the app.
  2. We check them as our client terms describe (the register, VIES, the licence, the insurance, the sanctions lists), when you join and again on a schedule (VIES monthly, the papers when they expire).
  3. Tell us of any change (a lapsed policy, a withdrawn licence, a new director) within 3 working days. Jobs stop on the day a paper expires.
  4. A false or altered document ends your account at once (section 15).

4. Insurance

  1. CMR liability insurance for every trip you take: it covers used cars, every country of the trip, and at least €50,000 per event (€250,000 for a vehicle with more than 3 car places).
  2. To carry cars worth more than €15,000: cover for each car's full value, up to the value band of the car. For those cars you answer to the client up to the car's value, written on the consignment note (CMR Article 24), and your full-value cover must pay it.
  3. Tell us at once if any cover lapses or shrinks.

5. Following the law

You alone are responsible for following the transport laws on every trip, and you keep every licence, registration and insurance they require. They include the driving and rest times and the tachograph; driving licences and, where needed, Code 95 and driver attestations; the Community licence and its certified copy in the vehicle; posting declarations and the host country's minimum pay where your drivers are posted; cabotage limits; road tolls; and Romania's e-Transport where it applies. Car2Door checks only the documents in section 3. Its check does not take over any of these duties.

6. Your mandate to Car2Door

By accepting these terms you appoint Car2Door, for the jobs you take through Car2Door Pro, to:

  1. offer and book the transport in your name, at the price section 9 sets, on the transport conditions (car2door.eu/transport-conditions);
  2. give the client, in your name, the information the law requires before and at booking;
  3. receive for you the client's instructions, reports and claims (a report in the app is notice to you);
  4. collect the client's payment as your collection agent: the client's payment to Car2Door counts as payment to you;
  5. refund the client as the cancellation rules say;
  6. issue your invoices and credit notes to the client in your name and on your behalf (section 11);
  7. hold a trip's payout while a report is open and pay the client from it when you or your insurer accept the claim (section 13).

7. How jobs are offered to you

  1. A job goes to transporters whose vehicles and papers fit it: the route and countries of your CMR cover, the sum insured, free car places and payload, a winch for a car that doesn't drive, full-value cover for a car over €15,000, and, for a private client, that you charge the pickup country's VAT (section 10).
  2. Transporters with a planned run on the route see it first. Then all who fit see it at the same time. The first to accept gets it. Ratings do not change who sees a job.
  3. A new account takes cars worth up to €15,000 for its first 3 clean deliveries. No vehicle carries more than €250,000 of Car2Door cars at a time.
  4. You are free to accept or refuse any job and to work for anyone else. There is no minimum. Refusing or ignoring a job never changes which jobs you see, your rating or your access.
  5. You run your own business: your own vehicles, licence, insurance and staff, your own other customers, your own route and timing within the job's dates, and your own business risk.
  6. If we change how jobs are offered, we tell you 15 days before (section 17).

8. Doing the job

  1. Name the driver and the vehicle's plates in the app 24 hours before pickup, from your approved drivers and vehicles. Only that driver and vehicle carry the car. No subcontracting and no reloading onto another vehicle.
  2. At pickup: the driver asks for the 6-digit pickup code, shows an ID to whoever hands over, ticks that the VIN on the car is the one booked (no match, no loading), takes the four corner photos and a close-up of each damage mark with the app's camera, writes old damage in words, and signs with whoever hands over.
  3. The consignment note is the e-CMR in the app; you agree to its electronic signatures (the e-CMR Protocol). For a pickup in Belgium, print it and sign on paper.
  4. On the road: an open car transporter; the car locked, its keys never left in it; overnight only in a guarded or closed place; instructions only from the client, in the app.
  5. At delivery: only to the named recipient against the delivery code; the same four photos; the recipient signs. Wait at least 60 minutes after calling and messaging before you treat a contact as absent, and tell us in the app.
  6. Never ask the client for extra money, in cash or otherwise, and never offer a Car2Door client a trip outside the app.

9. Price, tariff and commission

  1. The tariff. The client's price is set by Car2Door's tariff, as the most you may charge. You accept the tariff in advance for every job you take. You may accept a job for less where the app offers it; the client then pays less.
  2. The client's price is yours. It is the price of your transport; you charge VAT on it as section 10 says.
  3. Car2Door's commission for each job is 15% of the client's net price, and at least €50 a car. When a job is hard to fill, we may lower it. Vouchers we give clients come out of our commission, not your price.
  4. On a client's late cancellation or no-show you get the part the client pays, and our commission is cut by the same share.
  5. Where you see it. The job card in Offers shows your payout. The commission is in these terms, on our commission invoice for each job, and under Earnings and Invoices.
  6. Our commission invoice: for a transporter in the Netherlands with 21% Dutch VAT; for every other transporter with the VAT reverse charged (VAT Directive Articles 44 and 196), against your VAT number or, without one, your national tax number.

10. VAT on your transport

  1. The VAT on your transport is yours to declare and pay.
  2. For a private client it is the VAT of the pickup country, from the first trip. You declare it through the EU's one-stop shop (OSS) in your own country, or with a VAT number in each pickup country. Only a transporter that tells us in the app that it does this gets private clients' jobs.
  3. A business client pays the net price with the VAT reverse charged, except a business in your own country, which pays your country's VAT.
  4. We pay you that VAT with the payout, and give you every quarter a summary of your trips per country for your OSS return.

11. Invoices in your name

  1. Mandate. You authorise Car2Door to issue, in your name and on your behalf, the invoice for each job to the client, and a credit note when a job is cancelled or part of the price is refunded. Each says it was issued by StepsNest B.V. (Car2Door) in your name and on your behalf, and that the client paid Car2Door as your collection agent.
  2. Your number series. These invoices carry their own series: TR-[your code]-[year]-[number], credit notes TRC-[your code]-[year]-[number]. The series is reserved for the invoices Car2Door issues for you; you don't use it yourself, and you keep your own series for your other invoices.
  3. Copies. Each invoice and credit note is under Invoices in Car2Door Pro and emailed to you. Keep them in your books and declare them in your VAT returns.
  4. The 14-day check. Check each invoice and credit note issued in your name, and each self-billed invoice (section 12). Tell us within 14 days of receiving it if anything is wrong; we then correct it with a credit note and a new invoice. After 14 days it counts as accepted by you, which doesn't stop a correction the law requires.
  5. Poland. A Polish transporter gives Car2Door, with these terms, its authorisation (upoważnienie, art. 106d ust. 2 of the Polish VAT Act) to issue invoices in its name and on its behalf.
    • Business clients. A business client's invoice must go through KSeF, also for a client abroad with the VAT reverse charged. You issue it in KSeF yourself, from the details the app gives you under Invoices you issue yourself, and enter its number there; we send the client a receipt for its payment. A correction or credit note to it is yours too, quoting its KSeF number.
    • Private clients: we issue the invoice outside KSeF in your TR series.
    • Tell us at once if your VAT status changes (active, exempt, VAT-UE, OSS).
  6. Romania. Romanian invoicing rules apply to every invoice we issue for a Romanian transporter (Codul fiscal art. 319).
    • Before your first job: tell your tax office by registered letter that StepsNest B.V. (Car2Door, Weert, the Netherlands, with the VAT number under Company details below) issues invoices in your name, as Romanian law asks of you.
    • Through RO e-Factura: an invoice the law needs to go through e-Factura (the app lists each one under Invoices you issue yourself). You issue and upload it yourself, from the details the app gives you, and enter its number there; we send the client a receipt for its payment. A storno or correcting invoice to it is yours too.
    • Outside e-Factura: we issue the invoice in your TR series and email it to the client.
    • Without a VAT number (PFA, II): give us your special VAT code (art. 317) before you take a business client's job abroad or receive our commission invoice.
  7. Other national systems (such as Belgium's Peppol, Croatia's eRačun and fiscalisation, Hungary's Online Számla): where one needs the invoice to come from you, you issue it there yourself in the same way, and we send the client a receipt for its payment.
  8. You stay responsible for your invoices and VAT. We write them from the details you give us, so keep your company name, address and VAT number right in the app.

12. Share & Earn (not offered at present)

  1. Share & Earn and partner commissions are not offered at present. When we pay you such a reward, we issue the invoice for it ourselves, as your customer (self-billing, VAT Directive Article 224). You agree to this.
  2. Self-billed invoices carry their own series and are under Invoices. You accept each one unless you tell us within 14 days that something is wrong (section 11.4).
  3. You may end self-billing by telling us in writing; rewards are then paid against your own invoice.

13. Payouts and holds

  1. We pay your payout through Stripe to a bank account in your company's own name, 48 hours after the delivery code, once the four corner photos of both the pickup and the delivery have reached us. While you or the client have fewer than 3 paid trips, it waits 72 hours, as a guard against fraud. Your share of a cancelled trip (section 9.4) pays out 24 hours after the cancellation, so that a mistaken cancellation can be undone first.
  2. You set up payouts with Stripe and accept Stripe's Connected Account Agreement. Bank details change only through the app or Stripe, and a change is checked before the next payout.
  3. Holds. Each hold has a reason, shown in the app:
    • a damage report the client makes before the payout holds that trip's payout for up to 30 days;
    • missing tax details for the EU's DAC7 report: after two reminders and 60 days, payouts wait until they come;
    • a payment dispute the client's bank opens about your transport: we may take the disputed amount back from later payouts if the bank decides against it;
    • when the company on your Stripe payout account differs from the one you gave us, your payouts wait until a person on our team checks it; your new jobs go on unless that person pauses them;
    • when your company details change after we approved them, your payouts and the invoices in your name wait until a person on our team checks the change; your new jobs go on;
    • while a person on our team holds your account's payouts (a suspension, or while we look into signs of fraud), new jobs pause too; trips under way go on.
  4. Claims. We send you a client's report the same day. Answer in the app within 2 working days and tell your CMR insurer yourself; we don't contact insurers. If you or your insurer accept the claim within the hold, we pay the client from the held payout and tell you. After the hold ends, the payout goes to you and the claim goes on between you, the client and your insurer.

14. Giving a job back

  1. Free until 48 hours before the pickup window. After that it costs €25, taken off your next payouts after 24 hours (so a mistake can be waived first).
  2. The job goes back to other transporters and we tell the client.
  3. Our counters only flag repeated give-backs. A person on our team decides any pause (section 15).

15. Ratings, pauses and ending

  1. After a completed trip the client may rate the driver and your company, 1 to 5 stars with a comment. Your average shows on your profile and to clients. We don't remove a review for a low rating; you can report one in the app.
  2. Who decides. A person on our team decides every pause, restriction or closing of your account that rests on our judgement. Our signals only flag your account for that person: late give-backs and no-shows (3 in 90 days, or 5 in 12 months), a company on your Stripe payout account that differs from yours, company details changed after approval, and a company register's answer. The payout holds in section 13.3 start by the fixed rules written there, as a safety step until a person looks; a person then decides whether one goes on. That person tells you the reasons in writing, in the app and by email, by the day the decision takes effect, with the name of the person you can talk to. You can ask for the decision to be reviewed by another person, with your side of it; we answer in writing within 2 weeks. You can also take it to court (section 20).
    A few steps happen without a person, because a law, a fact or your own step decides them, not our judgement:
    • an expired or missing paper stops new jobs until you upload a valid one (section 3.3): checking papers is our duty, and only you can renew them;
    • a possible match on a sanctions list stops jobs and payouts until a person on our team clears it: sanctions law forbids making money available to a listed person;
    • new terms: new jobs wait until you accept them (section 17), because each job is booked on the terms in force;
    • Stripe disabling your payouts: that is Stripe's own decision, and we can pay nothing without it;
    • a company outside the EU gets no jobs: only EU transport businesses may join (section 2.1);
    • a VAT number VIES no longer knows stops counting for its country, and we tell you: a reverse-charged invoice needs a valid number;
    • the demo account we give app store reviewers gets no payouts: it is ours, not a transport company.
    A person on our team looks at any of them when you ask: tap "Ask a person to look" in the app, or write in the chat.
  3. We may restrict or pause your account, or stop offering you jobs, for: an expired or missing document; a false or altered document; a sanctions hit; signs of fraud; asking a client for extra money or taking our clients outside the app; repeated late give-backs or no-shows; a safety risk; or a legal duty.
  4. Ending. You can stop at any time and delete your account in the app; finish or give back your accepted jobs first (section 14). The account is then frozen at once and deleted after 28 days, or at once if you choose "Delete now". If a payout or an invoice is still due on that day, or a damage report or a payment dispute about one of your trips is open, we delete what can go then and the rest when it is settled or closed, and tell you within one month what was done and what waits (Privacy Policy, section 11). We end the account with 30 days' notice and our reasons, or at once for a legal duty or a serious or repeated breach of these terms (including false documents).

16. Your data, and the data you get

  1. How we use your and your drivers' data is in our Privacy Policy. Give your drivers our driver notice (car2door.eu/privacy#drivers).
  2. For the client's, the seller's and the recipient's names, phone numbers and addresses you get for a job, you are a separate controller under the GDPR. Use them only for that job and your legal duties (such as your tax records and the consignment note), never for marketing, and delete them when your law lets you.
  3. What you can see in the app: your jobs, invoices, payouts, documents and ratings, during your account and for as long as the law makes us keep them. You can ask us for a copy of your data, your ratings and reviews included, free of charge. We report your payouts to the Dutch tax office for the EU's DAC7 rules.
  4. What the app records, and what it never does. It shares your driver's latest position from the way to an accepted pickup until delivery, keeps no trail, shows the client and the recipient only the kilometres left in transit, the exact point only in the last 30 km before delivery, and nothing during rests, and deletes it at delivery. It records photos and signatures only at the handovers. It records ratings, give-backs, document dates and the checks in section 3. We never process emotions, private conversations, health, union membership or face matching, and nothing while you are not doing a Car2Door job. Our privacy risk assessment of these systems is available to you on request; we review them at least every 2 years.

17. Changes to these terms

We tell you about a change in the app and by email at least 15 days before it applies. You can end your account before then. You accept the new version in Car2Door Pro at Profile › Papers before you take new jobs; trips under way finish on the old version. No change applies to the past.

18. Your complaints

Write in the app's chat or to support@car2door.eu. A person answers, usually within 2 working days.

19. Liability between us

  1. You answer for your carriage under the CMR. If a client or an authority holds Car2Door liable for your carriage or your breach of the law, you indemnify us.
  2. We answer for our own duties in these terms. Our liability to you is limited to the commission on the job concerned, except for intent or gross negligence.

20. Law and courts

Dutch law. The court in Limburg (Rechtbank Limburg), the Netherlands. These terms are in English; when they are published in your language, each version is equally valid.
